Quranic Purity: We have compiled these instances purely from the Noble Quran. Every phrase is rendered in authentic Rasm al-Uthmani.
Graduated Progression: The instances are structured carefully from 5-12 syllables to help you build your recitation skills gradually.
Focused Scope: This lesson isolates a single rule to provide you with an undistracted, immersive study experience. Read, record, and await your teacher's feedback!
