It was narrated about the Prophet ﷺ
أَلَا إِنَّ الدُّنْيَا مَلْعُونَةٌ مَلْعُونٌ مَا فِيهَا إِلَّا ذِكْرَ اللهِ وَمَا وَالَاهُ وَعَالِمٌ أَوْ مُتَعَلِّمٌ
"Verily, the world is cursed and cursed is all that is in it, except the remembrance of Allah and what is associated with it, a scholar, or a student of knowledge."
