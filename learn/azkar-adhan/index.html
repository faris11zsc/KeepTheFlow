Azkar of the Adhan

Three powerful supplications to say after hearing the call to prayer

O Allah, Lord of this perfect call and established prayer, grant Prophet Muhammad al-Waseelah
الوسيلة
The highest station in Paradise, granted only to one servant of Allah — and the Prophet ﷺ hoped it would be him.
Sahih Muslim 384
 and al-Fadeelah
الفضيلة
A rank of extra virtue and honor above all creation.
Sahih al-Bukhari 614
 , and resurrect him to the Praiseworthy Station
المقام المحمود
The station of Grand Intercession on the Day of Judgment, where the Prophet ﷺ will intercede for all of humanity.
Surah Al-Isra 17:79
 that You have promised him. 📖 Bukhari 614
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ هَذِهِ الدَّعْوَةِ التَّامَّةِ وَالصَّلَاةِ الْقَائِمَةِ، آتِ مُحَمَّدًا الْوَسِيلَةَ وَالْفَضِيلَةَ، وَابْعَثْهُ مَقَامًا مَحْمُودًا الَّذِي وَعَدْتَهُ
Why this du'a is special
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever says this du'a after hearing the adhan, my intercession will be guaranteed for him on the Day of Judgment." This is the only du'a in Islam with a direct promise of the Prophet's intercession attached to it. Narrated in Sahih al-Bukhari (614) through Jabir ibn Abdullah رضي الله عنه.
I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Muhammad ﷺ as my messenger, and with Islam as my religion. 📖 Muslim 386
رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ رَسُولًا، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا
The reward for this dhikr
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever says this when the muezzin finishes, his sins will be forgiven." (Sahih Muslim 386). This short yet powerful declaration renews your covenant with Allah — affirming your satisfaction with His lordship, His religion, and His final messenger.
There is no power, nor strength
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ
Hawl (حَوْل) = the ability to change one's state. Quwwah (قُوَّة) = the power to sustain that change. Together: no transformation or endurance is possible without Allah.
Ibn Qayyim — Al-Fawa'id
 except through Allah. 📖 Bukhari 613
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
A treasure of Paradise
The Prophet ﷺ told Abu Musa al-Ash'ari رضي الله عنه: "Shall I not guide you to a treasure from the treasures of Paradise? Say: La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah." (Sahih al-Bukhari 6384, Sahih Muslim 2704). This is said when the muezzin says "Hayya 'ala as-salah" and "Hayya 'ala al-falah."
